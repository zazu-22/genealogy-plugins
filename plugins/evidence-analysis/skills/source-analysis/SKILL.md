# Source Analysis

Expert knowledge for evaluating genealogical sources and evidence using the methodology developed by Elizabeth Shown Mills.

## When This Skill Applies

- Classifying sources as original or derivative
- Determining if information is primary, secondary, or indeterminate
- Evaluating whether evidence is direct, indirect, or negative
- Assessing the reliability and weight of genealogical evidence
- Building proof arguments from multiple sources

## Core Framework

### The Three-Level Analysis

Every genealogical source must be analyzed at three levels:

| Level | Question | Categories |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Source** | Is this an original or a copy? | Original, Derivative, Authored |
| **Information** | Who provided this? When? | Primary, Secondary, Indeterminate |
| **Evidence** | Does this answer my question? | Direct, Indirect, Negative |

### Key Principle

> "A source is not inherently 'good' or 'bad.' The quality of any source depends upon the circumstances of its creation and the nature of the information it provides."
> — Elizabeth Shown Mills

## Quick Classification Guide

### Sources

| Type | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Original** | First recording of information | Handwritten church register |
| **Derivative** | Copy, transcription, or abstract | Published marriage index |
| **Authored** | Analysis, narrative, compilation | Family history book |

### Information

| Type | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Primary** | From someone with firsthand knowledge | Mother reporting child's birth |
| **Secondary** | From someone reporting hearsay | Neighbor stating birth year |
| **Indeterminate** | Informant unknown or unclear | Undated, unsigned record |

### Evidence

| Type | Definition | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Direct** | Explicitly states the answer | Birth certificate states birthdate |
| **Indirect** | Must be combined with other evidence | Age at marriage implies birth year |
| **Negative** | Expected information is absent | Name missing from tax list |

## Analysis Process

1. **Identify the source** → Original, Derivative, or Authored?
2. **Identify the information** → Who provided it? When?
3. **Identify the evidence** → Does it answer your question?
4. **Weigh the evidence** → Consider all factors together
