# Testing Platforms

Overview of major DNA testing platforms and their features.

## Major Testing Companies

### AncestryDNA

**Website**: ancestry.com/dna

**Test Type**: Autosomal only

**Strengths**:
- Largest database (20+ million tests)
- Most matches for genealogy
- Integration with Ancestry family trees
- ThruLines feature suggests common ancestors

**Limitations**:
- No Y-DNA or mtDNA
- No chromosome browser
- Requires subscription for full tree features

**Best For**: Finding cousins, general genealogy research

### 23andMe

**Website**: 23andme.com

**Test Type**: Autosomal (+ basic Y and mtDNA haplogroups)

**Strengths**:
- Health reports (with Health+Ancestry option)
- Good ethnicity estimates
- Chromosome browser available
- Second largest database

**Limitations**:
- Less genealogy-focused
- Fewer users with family trees
- Basic Y/mtDNA (haplogroups only, not matching)

**Best For**: Combined health and ancestry interests

### FamilyTreeDNA (FTDNA)

**Website**: familytreedna.com

**Test Types**: Autosomal, Y-DNA, mtDNA (all three)

**Strengths**:
- Only company offering all three test types
- Best Y-DNA testing (multiple levels)
- Best mtDNA testing
- Chromosome browser
- Free uploads from other companies

**Limitations**:
- Smaller database than Ancestry
- Interface less intuitive

**Best For**: Y-DNA and mtDNA research, detailed analysis

### MyHeritage DNA

**Website**: myheritage.com/dna

**Test Type**: Autosomal only

**Strengths**:
- Strong international database
- Good ethnicity estimates
- Chromosome browser
- Theory of Family Relativity feature

**Limitations**:
- Smaller database than Ancestry
- Some features require subscription

**Best For**: International ancestry, European research

## Third-Party Analysis Tools

### GEDmatch

**Website**: gedmatch.com

**What It Does**:
- Accepts uploads from major testing companies
- Matches across company databases
- Advanced analysis tools

**Key Features**:
- One-to-many matching
- Chromosome browser
- Triangulation tools
- Admixture calculators

**Cost**: Free basic, paid for Genesis (larger matches)

**Best For**: Cross-platform matching, detailed segment analysis

### DNA Painter

**Website**: dnapainter.com

**What It Does**:
- Chromosome mapping tool
- WATO probability calculator
- Shared cM tool

**Key Features**:
- Paint chromosomes with known ancestors
- Visualize which DNA comes from which ancestor
- Calculate relationship probabilities

**Cost**: Free for basic use, subscription for full features

**Best For**: Visual chromosome mapping, relationship analysis

### Genetic Affairs

**Website**: geneticaffairs.com

**What It Does**:
- Auto-clustering of matches
- AutoTree building

**Key Features**:
- Automated grouping of related matches
- Identifies clusters of shared ancestors

**Cost**: Pay-per-use

**Best For**: Organizing large match lists

## Testing Strategy

### For Most Genealogists

1. **Start with Ancestry** (largest database)
2. **Download raw data**
3. **Upload to FTDNA and MyHeritage** (free)
4. **Upload to GEDmatch** (cross-platform matching)

### For Specific Questions

- **Paternal line**: Y-DNA at FTDNA
- **Maternal line**: mtDNA at FTDNA
- **Unknown parent**: Test at multiple companies
- **Adoptee research**: All major databases

## Raw Data and Transfers

### Downloading Raw Data

Most companies allow you to download your raw DNA data file.

**Format**: Usually a text file with genetic marker data

**Privacy note**: Raw data can be uploaded elsewhere—consider privacy implications.

### Transfer Options

| From | To | Cost |
|------|-----|------|
| AncestryDNA | FTDNA | Free |
| AncestryDNA | MyHeritage | Free |
| AncestryDNA | GEDmatch | Free |
| 23andMe | FTDNA | Free |
| 23andMe | MyHeritage | Free |
| 23andMe | GEDmatch | Free |

### Why Transfer?

- Access different databases
- Use different tools
- Find matches who tested elsewhere
- Backup your data

## Choosing Where to Test

### Consider:

1. **Research goals**: What question are you trying to answer?
2. **Family coverage**: Where have relatives already tested?
3. **Geography**: Some databases better for certain regions
4. **Features needed**: Chromosome browser? Tree integration?
5. **Budget**: Test once, transfer to others free

### Common Recommendations

| Goal | Recommended |
|------|-------------|
| Find cousins (U.S.) | Ancestry |
| Find cousins (Europe) | MyHeritage |
| Y-DNA research | FTDNA |
| mtDNA research | FTDNA |
| Comprehensive | Ancestry + upload to others |
| Unknown parentage | All companies |

## Privacy Considerations

### Before Testing

- Review each company's privacy policy
- Understand data sharing options
- Consider implications for family members
- Know how to delete data if desired

### Opt-Out Options

Most companies allow you to:
- Opt out of matching
- Limit who sees your data
- Delete your data
- Restrict research use

### Law Enforcement Considerations

Some databases have been used in criminal investigations. Check each company's policies if this concerns you.
