# Research Planning

Expert knowledge for planning and organizing systematic genealogical research.

## When This Skill Applies

- Formulating specific research questions
- Selecting appropriate records to search
- Understanding records available for specific localities
- Maintaining research logs and documentation
- Prioritizing research tasks

## Core Process

### The Research Cycle

```
1. QUESTION  →  What do I want to know?
       ↓
2. PLAN      →  What sources might answer it?
       ↓
3. SEARCH    →  Locate and examine sources
       ↓
4. ANALYZE   →  Evaluate what I found
       ↓
5. CONCLUDE  →  Answer the question (or refine it)
       ↓
   [Loop back with new questions]
```

## Research Question Formulation

### The SMART Framework

Good research questions are:

| Element | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **S**pecific | One focused question | "When did John die?" not "Tell me about John" |
| **M**easurable | Answerable with evidence | Can be proven true/false |
| **A**chievable | Records likely exist | Within record survival |
| **R**elevant | Advances your goal | Connects to larger research |
| **T**ime-bound | Defined scope | Specific time period |

### Question Types

| Type | Purpose | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Identity | Who is this person? | "Is John Smith of 1850 census the same as 1860?" |
| Relationship | How are they connected? | "Who were Mary's parents?" |
| Event | What happened and when? | "When and where did John and Mary marry?" |
| Location | Where was this? | "Where was John living in 1875?" |
| Context | What was the situation? | "What brought the family to Ohio?" |

## Record Selection

### Quick Decision Matrix

| Question | Primary Records | Secondary Records |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Birth | Vital records, church | Census, family Bible |
| Death | Vital records, obituary | Cemetery, probate |
| Marriage | License/certificate | Church, newspaper |
| Parentage | Birth records, census | Probate, DNA |
| Migration | Census sequence, land | Newspaper, church transfer |
